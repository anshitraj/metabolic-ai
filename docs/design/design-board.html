Before it thinks

It does the math first.

Every candidate model is priced before a single token is generated. The policy engine keeps the best model whose margin clears the floor for the current state.

  • Estimated cost shown before you send
  • Actual cost shown after, from provider usage
  • One written reason for every routing choice
Operating states

Four moods, all earned.

The organism's shape comes from the policy engine's state. Colour, size, filaments and orbit speed all change together.

Abundance

Healthy and generous

Margin ≥ 30%, liquidity ≥ 3× reserve. Premium models allowed when they clear margin.

Defensive

Tightening up

Margin under 20% or reserve thinning. Economy models preferred; costly features off.

Survival

Protecting the reserve

Liquidity at or below the hard reserve. Unprofitable inference blocked; clear funding message.

Recovery

Rebuilding carefully

Funds back, stability not yet proven. Capacity returns in steps; abundance after 5 stable minutes.
